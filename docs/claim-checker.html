Claim checker

What I build for a client: a pass that treats every headline number as a claim.

The job

A draft — a brief, a memo, a slide outline — goes through a checker before anyone presents it. The workflow walks the text, pulls each headline number, and asks one question: where is the sentence this came from?

Verbatim or UNVERIFIED

Each number needs a verbatim source sentence. Paraphrase is not enough. “About four” does not cover “4.3.” If the source is missing, the file is marked UNVERIFIED. The run does not round a 3.1 into a 4 to match the deck.

What I do not do

I do not let the model “approximately confirm” a figure. I do not score confidence. A number is sourced or it is not. The client can ship the file or they can fix the claim. Those are the two exits.

What the client sees

A marked draft: each headline number paired with a quoted sentence, or a red UNVERIFIED on the file. No quiet pass.

Clean-room site. No client files, workflow JSON, or keys.
