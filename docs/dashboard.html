An empty week showed a 4.3.

The ops board sits on top of the runs: boards that finished, research that landed, claims that failed the check. A quiet week has no rows. That is a fact, not a hole in the design.

The tile showed 4.3 anyway. Empty looks unfinished. Unfinished invites a default. A 4.3 makes the room relax. Nobody asked where the rows were, because the number looked like work.

The turn is to leave the panel blank and say so. No last month in this month’s tile. No average of a missing score. When there are no rows, the tile reads that there are no rows.

The new rule: an empty week says empty. A fake 4.3 is a finished-looking lie.
