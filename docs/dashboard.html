Ops dashboard

What I build for a client: a board on top of n8n runs that stays empty when the week is empty.

The job

Operators need to see what actually ran: leads that booked, research that finished, claims that failed the check. The dashboard reads those runs. It does not decorate them.

Honest empty

If the week has no closed deals, the panel is empty. A fake 4.3 looks finished and is a lie. I would rather ship a blank tile with the label “no rows this period” than a number that makes the room relax.

What I do not do

I do not seed sample metrics. I do not average a missing score into a default. I do not show last month’s figure in this month’s tile. Empty is a state, not a gap to fill.

What the client sees

Tiles that match the ledger. When there is nothing to show, they see nothing — and they know that is the truth.

Clean-room site. No client files, workflow JSON, or keys.
