The report named a winner on a tie.

They wanted a freeze of the field: who is on the floor, who is next to whom. A snapshot. Something a merchant could hold in a meeting without guessing.

The file named a winner. The scores were tied. A trophy had been handed out so the slide would have a headline. It also invented a supplier — a name pulled off a URL as if a path were a company. The snapshot was talking like a trend. It was one still frame, dressed as movement.

The turn is to refuse the prize. If you cannot prove a winner, you do not name one. If you only have one freeze, you say so. I will not let a model break a tie because the room wants a name.

The new rule: no winner you cannot prove. A snapshot is one freeze, not motion.
