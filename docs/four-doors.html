Work

Four people walked into the same form.

It was 28 August 2026. I had an n8n run open and a stub list, so nobody would get a real text. The front door was a form posting to a webhook. Four records came through. They were not a funnel. They were four people.

The first was in North Carolina and wanted a call. That is a book path: three texts, an appointment, then stop. In this demo the texts are logged. They are not sent. The status is logged_not_sent. The appointment is a stub. It is not on a live calendar.

The second was in South Carolina and wanted information. That is nurture. Keep-warm only. No book. No “while I have you on the line.”

The third was in Texas. Texas does not get a text. The state gate closes. Hard stop. A Zapier chain with a model in the middle would have been helpful. Helpful looks like a yes. Helpful is a message you cannot work, and then a day you have to explain.

The fourth was North Carolina again, and typed junk. Not a yes. Not a no. An unusable reply. This is the one that almost became a booked yes. The machine that invents a yes would have filled the calendar. The CRM would have said booked. No one would have shown up.

Mine did not. The door was handoff. No guessed yes. No text.

I ran all four doors that day. Every text logged_not_sent. Live finish — after we scope, not before — is their CRM (GoHighLevel) and real texts that include STOP. I do not send a file.

This is the walk. Twenty minutes. You watch the junk fail the right way.
