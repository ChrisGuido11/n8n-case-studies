The week was empty, so the panel said so.

An ops board sits on top of n8n runs. Leads that booked. Research that finished. Claims that failed the check. A quiet week has no rows. That is a fact, not a gap in the design.

Empty looks unfinished. Unfinished invites a number. Someone always has last month’s figure, or a default, or a 4.3 that makes the room relax. A fake 4.3 is a finished-looking lie.

I do not put last month in this month’s tile. I do not average a missing score into a default. The panel says there are no rows this period, and it stays that way until a run writes a row.

The client sees what actually ran. When nothing ran, they see nothing — and they know that is the truth. Honest empty is the product.
