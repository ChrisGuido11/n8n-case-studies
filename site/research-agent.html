Work

They asked for real products.

The merchandising brief was simple: research what is actually on the floor. Real items. Real links. A list in, notes out.

The file that came back looked like shopping. Store links, tidy as a bibliography. None of them had been fetched. Someone left off the =. The node sent the literal template instead of the data. The model never received the list. It invented the aisle.

That is the lie a skim will not catch. The report is finished. The click 404s. Then you are the person who published a store that does not exist.

The turn is a count. What went in is accounted for on the way out — kept, dropped with a reason, or failed. If the node sent a template, the run stops. I do not let a model fill the holes so the page looks done.

The new rule: never publish a link you did not fetch.
